# SubTandem v0.1.8

## Improvements

- Make Session failures shorter and more actionable, keep `Translation is off` and `Translations are running` stable, and avoid preparing or showing subtitle details while translation remains off after restarting IINA.
- Simplify Profile feedback with a single `Testing…` message, a concise permanent-deletion warning, and no internal work-limit summary in Session.
- Use consistent guidance for unsupported subtitle types and translation-service failures across Profile Test and Session, and provide a direct prompt when no translation service is enabled.

## Bug Fixes

- Prevent supported embedded subtitles from incorrectly showing preparation failure or Retry during cold starts and rapid media or subtitle-track changes.
- Show translations sooner for OpenAI-compatible Profiles during dense dialogue instead of waiting for the cue buffer to reach 60.
